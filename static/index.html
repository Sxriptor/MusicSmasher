Mashup Studio

Add two tracks by file or YouTube URL. Each one is separated into vocals and instrumental, then analyzed for BPM and key. The vocals from one go on top of the instrumental from the other, stretched and re-tuned to match.
Combination
What gets adjusted
Vocals → instrumental Recommended. The beat stays untouched; the acapella is stretched and re-tuned (formants preserved) to fit it.
Instrumental → vocals Vocals stay natural; the beat is re-tempoed and transposed. Good when the singer sounds odd after shifting.
Meet in the middle Both move halfway. Smallest change to each side when the tracks are far apart.
